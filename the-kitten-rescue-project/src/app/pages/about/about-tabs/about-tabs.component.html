Dedication
At The Kitten Rescue Project, we are dedicated to saving the lives of kittens and neonates in need, with the ultimate goal of finding them forever homes. We rescue abandoned, orphaned, and sick kittens and work tirelessly to provide them with the care, nutrition, and socialization they require to grow and develop.
Your Support
We call upon all animal lovers to join us in our mission to rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome orphaned kittens. Whether you can offer your time, your resources, or your support, your contribution can make a difference in the lives of these vulnerable creatures. Together, we can make a world where every kitten has a chance to grow up happy and healthy.
A Chance To Thrive
Our vision at The Kitten Rescue Project is a world where every orphaned kitten and neonate has the chance to thrive and find their forever home. We believe that no kitten should be left to suffer or die alone, and that every kitten deserves love, care, and protection.
No Longer Overlooked
We envision a future where orphaned kittens and neonates are no longer overlooked or forgotten due to lack of resources, but are instead given a chance to survive. We are committed to working towards this vision every day, and invite you to join us in making a difference in the lives of orphaned kittens everywhere.
A Furball Named Butters
Our story began with a tiny orange-and-white kitten named Butters, born in the most unexpected of places: a bathtub. His mother was a stray cat who had found her way to a kindhearted rescuer just in time. Safe indoors and finally cared for, she gave birth to five kittens. With help, warmth, and a little luck, she raised her babies until they were ready to begin lives of their own.
The Moment Everything Changed
When Jennifer first heard about the litter, one kitten stood out right away. Butters was small, scrappy, full of personality, and absolutely determined to be loved. From the moment she met him, he changed everything. He captured her heart, opened the door to the world of rescue, and sparked a lifelong passion for helping cats in need.
Becoming A Foster
That passion grew into years of fostering kittens through COFAS, a rescue in Athens, Georgia. With every litter, Jennifer learned more, loved more, and became increasingly drawn to the kittens who needed the most urgent care: newborns, bottle babies, and underage kittens too young to survive without specialized support.
The Kitten Rescue Project
That calling eventually led her to Atlanta, where she founded The Kitten Rescue Project. Today, The Kitten Rescue Project provides a safe haven for the tiniest and most vulnerable kittens. Through around-the-clock care, bottle feeding, careful monitoring, warmth, medical support, and socialization, these fragile babies are given the chance to grow, thrive, and become the cats they were always meant to be. What began with one beloved bathtub-born kitten has grown into a mission to give countless others the same chance at safety, love, and a future of their own. Every kitten's journey is different, but each one begins with the same promise: to help them find their way home, one paw at a time.
